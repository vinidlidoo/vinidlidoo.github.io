The moat stack vs. ChatGPT

The "ChatGPT can't do this" framing is partly true (RAG could approximate it). The defensibility is the stack of overlapping layers — not any single one of them:

1
Client-specific post-training
Models post-trained on each client's standards, playbooks, spec sheets, regulatory requirements — plus relevant local municipal codes and blueprints.
2
Vertical knowledge corpus
Structured for narrow point-of-work queries — torque specs, fitting compatibility, code references. Answer quality depends on the right corpus indexed, not raw model size.
3
Form factor + multimodal capture
Phone camera real-time = "a second pair of eyes." Meta smart glasses = hands-free, sees what the worker sees. Can't be delivered through a ChatGPT tab. >1B frames processed hints the vision pipeline is where most engineering goes.
4
Workflow integration
Scoping, real-time QC against spec, documentation automation are workflow products — not chat. Defect catch at point-of-work is where rework costs actually live.
5
Distribution as moat
Launch partners aren't logos — they're design partners contributing proprietary training data and installed base. AIM trade-school integration means future workers arrive pre-trained on the system.
