Welcome to Waza 👋

Thanks for helping test Waza.

What is Waza? We built Waza as a coach for physical skills: something that helps
you do things in the real world, with your hands, not on a screen. You can create your
own skills (so you can remind yourself later how something is done) or load skills that
others have made, and an AI coach that sees what you see and hears you guides you
through the task out loud, hands-free. When you are really stuck, you can bring a real
person into the session to watch your view and talk you through it. For the full story
of what Waza is and where it is headed, demo videos included, read the intro post:
Waza: An AI Coach for Your Hands.

Waza is an early-stage experiment at the intersection of voice agents, AI video
understanding, video editing, and first-person live coaching. We will use your feedback
and reactions to decide which direction to double down on next. Nothing you try is
"wrong": if something feels confusing or magical, we want to hear about it all.

This page gets you from your invites to a live coached session in a few minutes.

🚀 Get started

Waza is built around a hands-free point of view, so the test runs on Meta glasses
(Ray-Ban Meta or the newer Meta-branded lineup). Setup is two invites and a key:

  1. Install the app via TestFlight. Open
    testflight.apple.com/join/FF8Pzx8g on
    your phone, install Apple's free TestFlight app if prompted, then install
    Get Waza from inside it.
  2. Accept the Meta glasses invite. Vincent has already added the Meta account email
    you gave him to Waza's release channel, so Meta sends you an invite by email; accept
    it (it is also visible at
    wearables.meta.com/invites).
  3. Open the app and paste your API key. Vincent gives you a personal key (a long
    line starting with sk-). Tap the gear icon (top left), choose Set API key,
    paste it in, and tap Done. This is what powers the AI coach. You only do this
    once. The key is stored safely on your phone.
  4. Allow camera and microphone when the phone asks. Waza needs both to stream your
    view and let you talk.
  5. Connect your glasses. While wearing them, tap the source button (bottom left)
    and pick the Glasses source, then follow the on-screen prompts to register with
    the Meta AI app. When asked for camera permissions, select Always Allow. (No
    glasses handy? The phone's rear camera works too; see "Stream and share your view"
    below.)
  6. Go live. Tap the play button at the bottom to connect.

To stop, tap the red stop button at the bottom.

✨ What you can do

Get coached by the AI

The coach is a voice agent that watches your live view and talks with you, hands-free.
It joins automatically when you connect, so you can just go live and start talking.
The sparkle button (bottom right) does different things before and after you
connect:

  • Before you connect, the sparkle button opens the Skills list. Pick a skill
    there if you want to be coached through a specific activity; it rides into your
    session. Pick nothing and you get the general-purpose coach.
  • While you are live, the same button turns the coach off and on: gray with a red
    sparkle     means the coach is in, solid red means it is off. Tap it to dismiss the
    coach when you would rather work without it; tap again to bring it back.
  • Or just say what you want to do. You do not have to work the menu. Tell the coach
    your goal out loud ("I want to learn to tie a half-Windsor") and it finds the skill for
    you: it either opens that skill's page or opens the Skills list already filtered down to
    the likely candidates. You always tap Load Skill yourself to confirm, so the coach
    never switches skills on you.
  • Once the coach is in, just talk naturally. It hears you and sees: whatever your
    main camera source is showing (rear camera, glasses, screen, ...) is what the coach is
    looking at while you talk. Point your view at the thing you want help with.
  • Every skill comes with short demo clips: the coach can play you the clip for a
    step right on your screen while you work.

Create your own skills

The Skills list starts small: about a dozen skills at launch, so the exact thing you
want to do is probably not there yet. That is by design; teaching Waza a new skill is
one of the main things we want you to try, and it only takes a video of the task being
done:

  • From the home screen, tap the sparkle button to open the Skills list, then tap
    the ⊕ button (top
    right). Choose Record a demo to film it now, or Photo Library to use videos
    you already have. A demo does not have to be a single take: it can be made of
    several clips or files.
  • Narrate while you demonstrate. Say out loud what you are doing as you do it; the
    audio track is currently an important signal Waza uses to understand the task and
    build the skill.
  • Waza builds a skill draft from the video. This takes a few minutes; you can leave the
    app. Your draft appears under the Yours shelf in the Skills list, with a dot when
    it is ready.
  • Tap the finished draft to review it, give it a name, and publish. Published skills
    show up in the Skills list for every tester, so others can learn what you taught.

Pause and pick up later

Long tasks do not have to happen in one sitting, and you do not have to reach for the
phone to stop:

  • Ask the coach for a break. Say something like "let's take a break" and it ends the
    session for you. Pocket the phone; nothing keeps streaming.
  • Come back when you are ready. Waza sends you a notification after a spoken
    break: tap it, even from the lock screen, and you are straight back in. (The phone asks
    your permission for notifications the first time you pause this way.)
  • Or open the app. A banner at the top of the home screen offers to resume your
    last task. Tap it to continue, or discard it if you are done with that task.
  • Resuming picks up where you left off: the coach remembers the earlier conversation, so
    you do not have to re-explain what you were doing.
  • Coaching sessions also end on their own after 30 minutes; if you were not done,
    resume the same way and keep going.

Bring in a real person

Your live view is shareable with anyone, whether you want help or just another pair of
eyes on you performing the skill:

  • Tap the gear icon and choose Copy viewer link, then send that link to anyone
    by text, chat, or email. They open it in a browser: nothing to install on their end.
  • They watch your live view and can talk back to you: walk you through a step you
    are stuck on, or watch you work and give feedback.
  • They can even share their own camera into the session, so you can see them while
    you stream.

Stream and share your view

Tap the source button (bottom left) to switch what the coach and your viewers see:

  • Glasses (the glasses icon): your point of view, hands-free.
  • Rear camera (the camera icon): the world in front of you.
  • Front camera (the person icon): you, selfie style.
  • Your phone screen (the rectangles icon): useful for showing an app or a video.

You can switch sources at any time while you are live.

Record a session

You can keep a video of any session:

  • While live, tap the gear icon and choose Record session. Tap Stop recording
    when you are done (or just end the session).
  • The app then gives you a link to the video. Anyone with the link can watch it for
    72 hours; after that it expires.

⚠️ Good to know

A few honest rough edges while we test:

  • Full disclosure: coaching sessions are not fully private. Sessions are not
    recorded by default: video is only kept when you record it yourself with Record
    session    , and Vincent can see those recordings. For every coached session, though,
    Vincent can read the conversation transcript and see the still frames sent to
    the AI at each turn, and uses them to improve Waza during this test. Keep that in
    mind for what you point the camera at.
  • If the glasses get stuck or stop responding, fully power them off and on again
    (a complete power cycle), then reconnect.
  • If the app crashes, start a fresh session. Tap play to begin a new one rather than
    trying to rejoin the old link. Rejoining the old session does not work.

💬 Reporting problems

Found a bug or something confusing? Message Vincent directly (iMessage or WhatsApp) and
include:

  • What you did (the steps leading up to it).
  • What you saw (what went wrong).
  • Roughly when it happened (the time helps us find the session).

No need for screenshots or screen recordings: Vincent can look up the session from his
side (recording, coach transcript), so the rough time is usually enough. Thanks for
testing.
